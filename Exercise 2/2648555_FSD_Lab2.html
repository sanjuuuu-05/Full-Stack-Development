What You Get

Built for the team that can't afford to miss a crack.

Every feature exists to answer one question: which section needs attention first?

Know it's failing, not just that it might be.

Computer vision flags cracks and fractures the moment they appear in footage — not weeks later at the next scheduled walk-through.

Stop guessing which section to inspect first.

Every defect gets a severity score, so your crew's next stop is always the highest-risk section — not just the next one on the list.

Never miss the alert because no one checked the dashboard.

Automatic alerts reach maintenance leads directly the moment a high-risk defect is confirmed — no dashboard-refreshing required.

Watch risk trends before they turn into incidents.

Historical defect analysis shows which sections are deteriorating over time, so budget goes toward prevention, not just repair.

Get footage analyzed without buying new hardware.

Works with your existing inspection cameras and Track Recording Car footage — no proprietary sensors to install first.

Give your inspectors an answer they can defend.

Every risk score comes with the imagery and classification behind it, so maintenance decisions hold up under audit review.

Accuracy depends on footage quality and defect type, which is exactly what the pilot phase is for — we validate detection rates against your team's manual inspection findings before any full rollout, so you see real numbers before committing.
